Our Heritage

The Chronicle of Medusa

2018: The Dream & Conception

Medusa was born from a visionary dream to challenge standard fine dining conventions. Our founders travelled across Italy, sourcing vintage marble, cast Versace decorative accents, and custom hand-forged lighting templates to prepare the foundation of the region's most exclusive palatial dining experience.

2020: The Grand Opening

We officially opened our doors in the heart of Chandigarh, establishing a sanctuary of visual and culinary theater. Within months, Medusa became the premier destination for elite events, VIP celebrations, and gastronomic connoisseurs seeking high-society dining.

2023: Michelin-Class Recognition

Led by Culinary Director Chef Giovanni Vance, our kitchen brigade was honored with international recognition for their pioneering "contrast and harmony" cooking techniques, elevating regional Indian and Mediterranean ingredients to a premium global stage.

2026: The Modern Luxury Era

Embracing state-of-the-art technological details, we introduced virtual interactive table layouts, real-time booking, and a bespoke customer loyalty tier system, ensuring that every guest receives customizable VIP service tailored perfectly to their individual preference.

Curiosities

Secrets of Medusa

Why the name "Medusa"?

Inspired by the classical symbol of allure and theatrical beauty in Italian high-fashion and classical mythology. Just as the mythical figure captures absolute attention, we aim to captivate your senses from the moment you step through our golden entrance arches, making the dining experience completely unforgettable.

Do you accommodate customized organic dietary requests?

Absolutely. Our culinary brigade, led by Chef Vance, regularly designs custom degustation menus for gluten-free, vegan, kosher, and specific organic preferences. We believe luxury is personal; kindly mention your requests when making your table reservation.

Can I host private events in the Vault Cellar?

Yes. The Vault Cellar is available for private bookings of up to 20 guests, offering an exclusive multi-course degustation menu paired with select reserve wines. Please contact our VIP hospitality hosts at least 48 hours in advance for reservations.

Allow us to curate your perfect gastronomic affair. Table reservations at Medusa are highly exclusive, ensuring an intimate atmosphere and bespoke service for every party.
