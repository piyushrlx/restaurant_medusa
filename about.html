Luxury Gastronomy

The Legend of Medusa

A sanctuary of epicurean luxury, blending timeless theatrical design with state-of-the-art culinary craft.

A Heritage of Palatial Dining

Founded with a bold vision to challenge standard fine dining conventions, Medusa Restaurant stands as Chandigarh's most exclusive culinary landmark. Every aspect of our dining spaces is inspired by classical Italian aesthetics, layered with the bold, theatrical energy of modern luxury fashion.

"We believe that a dinner at Medusa is not merely a meal. It is an act of sensory theater, curated to excite every element of your aesthetic sensibilities."

From our custom-cast Versace decorative elements to the precise choice of ambient warm lighting, we have designed a palatial environment where conversations flow as naturally as our premium wines. We invite you to sit back, lose track of time, and immerse yourself in Chandigarh's premier gourmet retreat.

Our Pure Dedication

To maintain our reputation, we commit unconditionally to two core principles: sourcing only the finest Grade-A ingredients from around the globe, and training our hosts to provide bespoke, intuitive service tailored perfectly to your individual pace.

The Culinary Director

Chef Giovanni Vance

Executive Chef & Culinary Director

With over 18 years of experience leading Michelin-recognized kitchens across Rome, London, and New Delhi, Chef Vance leads the culinary brigade at Medusa with raw artistic focus. His unique style centers on transforming traditional recipes by pairing them with unexpected textures and modern presentations.
